## First Encounter

Hi there. This might be the first time you've seen my website.

As a soon-to-be high school student, I can already anticipate that the next three years of content uploads will be unstable.

Because I'm fairly capable, I got into a top-tier class at No.1 High School (can't disclose the exact curriculum), and I'll need to head to school for classes soon.

I've long been tired of Shandong's way of doing things, but under the circumstances I have to bow to reality — so I can only find some comfort within it.

Even so, I still believe I can balance studying while keeping up my hobby of uploading videos. At first, I also thought that meeting high school would mean saying goodbye to my identity as an uploader. But given how I think, I won't let that happen. Without some kind of hobby, life would be so dull — and applied to high school life, that might even lead to mental health issues. Since I had the ability to get into this class, I believe I can find that balance too.

**I will never say goodbye because of this.**
