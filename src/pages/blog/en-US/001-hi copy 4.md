Hi. This might be the first time you see my website.

As a soon-to-be high school student, I can already anticipate that the next three years of content uploads will be unstable.

Because I am fairly capable, I was admitted to a top-tier class in a key high school, and I will need to go to school for training soon.

I have long been tired of the way things work in Shandong, but under objective conditions I have to bow to reality, so I can only seek some comfort in it.

Even so, I still believe I can balance studying and uploading videos. At first, I also thought that meeting high school would mean saying goodbye to my identity as an uploader. But based on my way of thinking, I will not let that happen. If I didn’t have some hobby, life would be so boring — and applying that to high school life could lead to some psychological problems. Since I have the ability to get into this class, I believe I can find that balance as well.

**I will never say goodbye because of this.**
